# Changelog

A lot has changed since the version you played before! Here's the full rundown.

## A Whole New Difficulty Selection Screen

The difficulty screen has been completely redesigned. Instead of a simple list, you now get a sidebar on the left with **Custom** as the main option, and the classic presets (**Standard**, **Classic**, **2 Player**) grouped underneath as quick-pick presets. Every mode is a click away.

### The Presets

Eight built-in difficulties to choose from, each faster and tighter than the last:

- **Easy** and **Medium** — relaxed, no coins, great for learning the game
- **Hard**, **Hardcore**, **Impossible**, **Nightmare**, **Hell** — progressively faster obstacle speed and shorter gaps between obstacles, earning you coins the harder you go (the harder the difficulty, the more points you need per coin — but the challenge and bragging rights scale up too)
- **Dynamic** — starts out slow and gentle, but never stops speeding up. There's no cap: it keeps accelerating forever, for as long as you can survive. How far can you actually get?

Starting at **Hardcore** and above (and once Dynamic ramps up past a certain speed), obstacles don't just come at you from the ground anymore — they also start **falling from above**, forcing you to watch both the floor and the sky at the same time.

### Brand New: Custom Mode

This is the big one. Instead of picking a fixed difficulty, you can now build your own with sliders and toggles:

- **Speed slider** — set the obstacle scroll speed to exactly how fast you want
- **Obstacle pace sliders** — control the minimum and maximum pause between obstacles yourself
- **1-Ups toggle** — decide whether extra-life powerups spawn at all
- **Vertical Obstacles toggle** — turn on the "falling from above" obstacles seen in the harder presets, independent of your chosen speed
- **Wind (Sides)** — a new mechanic: gusty horizontal wind that pushes you and the obstacles left and right. It's not constant — it builds up, gusts hard, calms down, and changes direction on its own throughout the round, so no two rounds of windy Custom feel the same
- **Wind (All Sides)** — everything Wind (Sides) does, plus a second wind axis blowing up and down. Wind from above will slow your falls and stretch out your jumps (you get "carried" by it); wind from below drags you down faster. It affects falling obstacles too, both their fall speed and how much they drift sideways
- **Free Movement (Beta)** — the most different mode yet. Turn this on and jumping/gravity disappear entirely — you move freely in all directions with WASD, like a top-down dodger. And obstacles no longer only come from the right: they fly in from all four edges of the screen, so you have to watch everywhere at once

Custom mode doesn't earn coins (to keep the reward economy fair — since you control the difficulty yourself), but it's there purely for however you like to play.

## Progression & Rewards

- **Power Roll shop**: spend coins on single rolls or x10 rolls for random powers, with a proper rarity system (Common through Mythic). Rolling a rare power now comes with a satisfying glowing reveal, and the results screen shows your x10 pulls sorted best-first with clear rarity badges
- **Cosmetic Shop**: unlock and equip trail effects and player skins, browsable with live animated previews before you buy
- **Achievement system**: unlock achievements for milestones (surviving hardcore runs, collecting powerups, hitting score thresholds, and more) for coin rewards, with popups when you unlock one and a dedicated screen to track your progress by category — currently switched off while we keep polishing it, but it'll be back
- **Profiles**: multiple people can now share the same install with their own separate save — stats, settings, and unlocks are all kept per-profile

## 2-Player Mode

Play on the same screen with a friend — **Co-op** (survive together) or **VS** (last one standing wins). Player 1 uses WASD/Space, Player 2 uses the arrow keys. Includes a rematch button and clear visual feedback when a player gets hit or eliminated.

## Statistics

The stats screen now tracks a lot more about how you play: total jumps, total game overs, and total coins earned over your lifetime (separate from your current coin balance). Your average score per round is now broken out **per difficulty** instead of one blended number, so you can actually see how you're doing on each difficulty individually.
